# Winwood playlist brief

I want a playlist that follows Steve Winwood through his whole career — the thread is *him*,
not any one band. Start with the Spencer Davis Group years (he was the teenage voice and
organ on "Gimme Some Lovin'" and "I'm a Man"), move through Traffic ("Dear Mr. Fantasy",
and the title track of "The Low Spark of High Heeled Boys", plus the whole *John Barleycorn
Must Die* album), the short-lived Blind Faith supergroup ("Can't Find My Way Home" and
"Presence of the Lord"), and finish with his 80s solo hits ("Higher Love", "Roll With It").

Only count tracks and records he actually performed on. Studio takes only — no live
versions. And no compilations or "best of" repackagings; I want the songs as they first
appeared on their original studio releases.
